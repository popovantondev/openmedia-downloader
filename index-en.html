A Swift app with a Python backend (yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Deno). There is no ready-made app yet; steps follow “Architecture and build”.

Requires: a Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later, Swift 6 (Xcode or Command Line Tools)
swift build
swift test
This builds and tests only the Swift components – no complete app, no real downloads

Connection

The app runs locally on your Mac and downloads media directly from the respective platform, such as YouTube or Vimeo. There is no cloud service of its own. Cookies are optional; public media can also be tried without cookies.
