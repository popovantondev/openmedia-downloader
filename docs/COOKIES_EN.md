# Cookies and sign-in

[User guide](https://popovantondev.github.io/openmedia-downloader/Guide-en.html) · [Deutsch](COOKIES.md) · [Русский](COOKIES_RU.md)

Cookies are optional. For public media, try “Continue without cookies” first. Browser sign-in does not establish permission to download a particular item.

To read Safari cookies, **OpenMedia Downloader 5.5 itself**, not Safari, needs Full Disk Access. After changing the permission, quit the app completely with ⌘Q and reopen it. Chrome and other browsers may request Keychain permission. Use only your own browser profile.

Cookies and tokens are confidential. Do not attach a browser database, cookie files, private links or a complete log to a bug report. Use a public example and a sanitized error message.

If sign-in fails, try a public link without cookies and read [troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING_EN.md). A successful cookie-read check establishes readability, not access to a video.

No ready-to-use app package is currently distributed: see [release status](RIGHTS_EN.md). The app does not bypass DRM, paid access or account restrictions.
